ENERGY MONTHLY – COMPLETE INSIGHT PAGE FRAMEWORK

GOAL
Transform monthly energy datasets into a single-page infographic that answers:
- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- Is it improving or worsening?
- How does it compare historically?
- Where is it heading?
- How does it compare with the EU?

=================================================
1. HEADLINE OF THE MONTH
=================================================
Purpose: Show the single most important energy story.
Examples:
- Renewables surpassed fossil fuels.
- Electricity exports reached a record high.
- Gas import dependency fell to a record low.

2. KPI SCORECARD
=================================================
Renewable Share (%)
= (RA100+RA200+RA300+RA400+RA500_5160)/TOTAL*100

Fossil Share (%)
= (C0000+G3000+O4000XBIO)/TOTAL*100

Electricity Trade Balance
= EXP - IMP

Gas Import Dependency (%)
= Net_imp/(IPRD+Net_imp)*100

User takeaway:
Quick understanding of transition, trade and dependency.
